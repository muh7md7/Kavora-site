FAQ

Straight answers.

What's the difference between ready-made and custom?

Ready-made solutions are proven agents or workflows we configure for your business — faster to launch. Custom agents are designed and built from scratch around your specific process.

Can I request an AI agent that isn't listed?

Yes. The examples are not limits. Describe what you need and we'll tell you honestly whether it's feasible and how we'd build it.

Which tools can it connect to?

It depends on the tools you use and whether they offer integrations. We confirm compatibility during scoping — we don't promise it upfront.

How is it priced?

Every solution gets a custom quote. Some have a one-time setup cost, some also have ongoing costs for hosting, AI usage, messaging or support. Third-party costs are paid by you directly and listed in the proposal before you commit. Ready-made solutions with a confirmed price are shared on request.

Can I buy online?

Not yet. Send an enquiry and we'll reply with availability, options and a quote.
