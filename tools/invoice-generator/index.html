How to make an invoice that gets paid on time

An invoice is more than a bill. It is the document your client's accounts team checks before releasing money, so anything missing slows payment down. A complete invoice answers four questions at a glance: who is asking to be paid, who should pay, what for, and by when.

What every invoice should include

  • A unique invoice number. Use a simple sequence such as INV-2611-01 so you and your client can refer to it in emails and bank transfers.
  • Issue date and due date. "Due on receipt" is vague; a real date (for example 7 or 14 days after issue) is easier to enforce.
  • Your details and your client's details. Business name, address and a contact email. Add your tax registration number if you are registered for VAT or sales tax.
  • Clear line items. Describe the work in words your client will recognise, with quantity and unit price, so nobody has to ask what a number means.
  • Tax and discount shown separately. Clients need to see the tax amount on its own line for their own records.
  • How to pay. Bank account, IBAN, payment link or wallet details in the notes field.

How to use this generator

  1. Fill in your business details and your client's details.
  2. Add each product or service as a line with quantity and price. The total updates as you type.
  3. Add a tax rate (for example 5% VAT in the UAE) and any discount.
  4. Press Download PDF and choose "Save as PDF" in the print window.

Your last invoice is remembered in this browser, so next time you only need to change the client, the date and the line items.

Tips for getting paid faster

Send the invoice the same day the work is delivered, while the value is fresh. Put the total and due date in the email subject line. For repeat clients, agree payment terms once in writing and reuse them. If an invoice is late, a short, friendly reminder on the due date works better than waiting a month.

Frequently asked questions

Is this invoice generator really free?

Yes. There is no sign-up, no watermark and no limit on the number of invoices.

Where is my invoice data stored?

Only in your own browser. Nothing you type is sent to our servers.

How do I save the invoice as a PDF?

Press Download PDF, then choose Save as PDF as the printer. On a phone, use the share or print option and save to Files.

Can I add VAT or sales tax?

Yes. Enter the tax rate as a percentage and it is calculated on the total after any discount.
