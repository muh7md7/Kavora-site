WhatsApp link and QR code generator

Why a WhatsApp link beats "DM us"

Every extra step between a customer and a conversation loses people. Asking them to save your number, open WhatsApp and type a message is three steps. A click-to-chat link opens the chat with your business straight away, with the first message already written, so they only have to press send.

Where to use your link

  • Instagram and TikTok bio, as your main link or inside a link-in-bio page.
  • Facebook and Instagram ads with a "Send WhatsApp message" goal, or as the website link.
  • Google Business Profile, your website header and email signature.
  • Printed QR codes on flyers, menus, shop counters, delivery bags and business cards.

How to write a pre-filled message that converts

Write it in your customer's voice and make it specific, so you know where the lead came from. For example: "Hi, I saw your Ramadan offer on Instagram. Is it still available?" Use a different message for each channel (ads, flyers, bio) and you will see at a glance which one brings the most chats.

Number format

Choose the country, then type the number without the leading zero. A Pakistani mobile 0300 1234567 becomes 92 300 1234567; a UAE mobile 050 123 4567 becomes 971 50 123 4567. The generator removes spaces, dashes and the leading zero for you.

Printing the QR code

Download the PNG and print it at least 2 × 2 cm (about 1 inch). Keep a white border around it and test it with two different phones before printing a large batch.

Frequently asked questions

Does the link work without saving my number?

Yes. wa.me links open a chat with any WhatsApp number without saving it as a contact.

Does it work with WhatsApp Business?

Yes. The link works for both WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business numbers.

Can I change the message later?

The message is part of the link, so create a new link if you want a different message. The QR code changes too.

Is the QR code free to print and use commercially?

Yes. It's a plain QR code with no tracking and no expiry.
