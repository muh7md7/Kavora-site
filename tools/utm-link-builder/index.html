UTM link builder

Tag your campaign links so you know exactly which post or ad brought each visitor. Free, no sign-up, and private: everything runs in your browser.

What UTM links do

When someone clicks a link to your website, analytics tools often can't tell whether they came from your Instagram story, a WhatsApp broadcast or a paid ad. UTM tags are small labels added to the end of the link that tell Google Analytics and most other tools exactly where the visit came from. They don't change the page; they only label the visit.

The five tags

  • utm_source: the platform, such as instagram, facebook, whatsapp or newsletter.
  • utm_medium: the type of traffic, such as social, paid_social, cpc, email or qr.
  • utm_campaign: the promotion, such as eid_sale or launch_week.
  • utm_term (optional): the keyword for search ads.
  • utm_content (optional): which version of the ad or post, such as video_a or carousel_b.

Rules that keep your reports clean

  1. Use lowercase everywhere. "Instagram" and "instagram" show up as two different sources. This builder converts to lowercase for you.
  2. Use underscores instead of spaces.
  3. Keep a naming list and reuse the same words every time.
  4. Never use UTM links inside your own website. They restart the visit and break your reports.

Printed QR codes

Set the medium to "qr" and the content to where the code is printed, such as "flyer" or "shop_counter". You'll finally know whether offline marketing brings visitors.

Frequently asked questions

Do UTM links affect SEO?

No. Search engines ignore UTM parameters on links shared in social media, ads and email.

Where do I see UTM results?

In Google Analytics 4, open Reports → Acquisition → Traffic acquisition and choose Session source / medium or Session campaign.

Can I shorten a UTM link?

Yes. Any link shortener works; the tags are kept when the short link redirects.

Which tags are required?

Source, medium and campaign are the essentials. Term and content are optional.
