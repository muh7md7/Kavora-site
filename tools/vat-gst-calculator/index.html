Adding tax and removing tax

There are two everyday questions. "What should I charge including tax?" Multiply the price by (1 + rate). A 1,000 AED service with 5% UAE VAT becomes 1,050 AED. "How much tax is inside this total?" Divide the total by (1 + rate) to get the amount before tax, and the difference is the tax. A 1,050 AED receipt contains 1,000 AED plus 50 AED VAT.

A common mistake is to take 5% off a tax-inclusive total. 5% of 1,050 is 52.50, not 50, so your records would be wrong by 2.50 on every sale. Always divide, never subtract a percentage.

Quick reference

  • United Arab Emirates: standard VAT rate 5%.
  • Saudi Arabia: standard VAT rate 15%.
  • Pakistan: general sales tax on goods is commonly 18%; services are taxed by the provinces at their own rates, so check which applies to you.
  • United Kingdom: standard VAT rate 20%.

Rates and rules change, and some goods and services are zero-rated or exempt. Use this calculator for quick numbers and quotes, and confirm the rate for your product with your tax authority or accountant.

Showing tax on invoices and price lists

Business customers usually want to see the price before tax, the tax amount and the total on separate lines, together with your tax registration number. Consumers usually expect the final price including tax. If you sell to both, show the tax-inclusive price prominently and the breakdown on the invoice. Our free invoice generator does this for you.

Frequently asked questions

How do I add 5% VAT to a price?

Multiply the price by 1.05. For example, 200 × 1.05 = 210.

How do I remove VAT from a total?

Divide the total by 1 plus the rate. For 5% VAT, divide by 1.05; the difference is the VAT.

What is the VAT rate in the UAE?

The standard rate is 5%. Some supplies are zero-rated or exempt.

Is this calculator accurate for filing tax returns?

It gives exact arithmetic, but you're responsible for using the correct rate for your product. Check with your tax authority or accountant.
