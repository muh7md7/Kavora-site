Questions

Who can join?

Customers, creators, agencies, freelancers and anyone with an audience of business owners or Mac users. You don't need to be a customer first.

How much can I earn?

30% of every sale you refer. On a $29 Kavora Desk licence that is $8.70 per sale. Commission applies to every product in the program.

How are sales tracked?

You get a personal link. When someone clicks it and buys, the sale is credited to you automatically by Lemon Squeezy, our payment partner.

When and how do I get paid?

Lemon Squeezy pays partners by bank transfer or PayPal on the 14th and 28th of each month, after a 30-day hold for refunds. Lemon Squeezy keeps a 2% fee from each commission.

Can I run ads with my link?

Yes, as long as you don't bid on the Kavora brand name or make claims we can't back up. Always disclose that you earn a commission.
