FAQ

Good questions.

Meta or Google — which do I need?

It depends on how your customers find you. Meta reaches people while they scroll; Google reaches people while they search. You can choose one or both.

Do you guarantee results?

No. Nobody honest can. We set campaigns up properly, test, optimise and report what actually happens.

Who pays for the ad spend?

Ad spend is paid directly to Meta or Google from your own account. Our management fee is separate.

Pricing. Custom quote after a short discovery call. Our fee covers our work. Third-party costs — ad spend, hosting and domains, AI usage, messaging and integration subscriptions — may apply and are listed in your proposal.
