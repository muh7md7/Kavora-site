How it runs

From brief to live.

Four steps. You see work at every one of them — no month of silence followed by a reveal.

  1. 01

    Map

    We watch how the work happens now, and find where the time actually goes.

  2. 02

    Design

    We write what the agent should say and do — and where it must stop and fetch a human.

  3. 03

    Build & test

    Built, connected to your tools, then tested against real cases before it meets a customer.

  4. 04

    Watch

    Live with alerts. We review the conversations and tighten the weak answers.
