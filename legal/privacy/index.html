Privacy policy

Last updated 28 October 2026

In short

We collect as little as possible, never sell your data and run no advertising trackers.

Accounts

If you create an account we store your name, email and sign-in method with Google Firebase Authentication. If you sign in with Google we receive your name, email and profile picture from Google. Passwords are handled by Firebase and never stored by us.

Waitlist

If you join the list we store your email address and the page you joined from, only to send product news. A hashed version of your IP address is kept for ten minutes to stop spam.

Purchases

Payments are processed by Lemon Squeezy, which receives your name, email, billing country and payment details under its own privacy policy. We receive your name, email and order details. Licence checks send your licence key and an anonymous device name to Lemon Squeezy.

Inside the app

Kavora Desk keeps widgets, notes, tasks and statistics on your Mac. Weather (Open-Meteo) and prayer times (AlAdhan) are requested using only the city and country you choose.

Cookies

We use only the storage needed to keep you signed in. No analytics or advertising cookies.

Your rights

Ask for a copy of your data, a correction or deletion at hello@kavora.com. Unsubscribe from emails in one click. You can delete your account at any time by emailing us.
