FAQ

Straight answers.

How we work, how pricing works and how to start.

Do I have to buy more than one service?

No. You can hire us for one service, several, or a complete solution. We'll only recommend what you need.

Do you work remotely?

Yes. Every project runs remotely — discovery, reviews and handover included.

Do you sell ready-made AI agents or only build custom ones?

Both. Where a ready-made solution fits, we set it up for you. Otherwise we design and build a custom agent.

Which advertising platforms do you manage?

Meta (Facebook and Instagram) and Google Ads. You can choose one or both.

Do you offer ongoing support?

Yes. Maintenance, content and optimisation can continue after launch.

How does pricing work?

Every project gets a custom quote after a short discovery conversation. We don't publish fixed prices because every scope is different. Where a ready-made AI solution has a confirmed price, we share it on request.

Are there costs beyond your fee?

Sometimes. Third-party costs — ad spend paid to Meta or Google, hosting and domains, AI usage, messaging or integration subscriptions — are paid by you directly and listed in the proposal before you commit.

How do we start?

Send an enquiry with a short description of what you need. We reply with questions and next steps.
